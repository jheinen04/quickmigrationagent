ADR-009: Disaster Recovery and Backup StrategyStatusProposedDate2026-09-08Decision MakersEnterprise ArchitectCloud Platform LeadBusiness Continuity ManagerSRE/Operations LeadContext and Problem StatementWhile Amazon Quick Sight is a fully managed serverless service with built-in high availability, organizations must still plan for disaster recovery (DR) of their BI assets — dashboards, datasets, analyses, themes, Q Topics, and user/group configurations. Quick Sight's identity management is tied to a specific identity region, which creates challenges for cross-region DR setups. Organizations must define their Recovery Time Objective (RTO) and Recovery Point Objective (RPO) and implement appropriate backup and recovery mechanisms.Decision DriversBusiness criticality of BI
