ADR-010: Asset Organization, Folder Structure, and Sharing ModelStatusProposedDate2026-09-08Decision MakersBI Platform LeadEnterprise ArchitectData Governance LeadBusiness Unit RepresentativesContext and Problem StatementAs Quick Sight deployments scale, the number of dashboards, analyses, datasets, and data sources grows rapidly. Without a deliberate organizational strategy, assets become difficult to discover, manage, and govern. Quick Sight provides folders (including shared folders in Enterprise edition), group-based sharing, and permission inheritance to organize and control access to assets.
