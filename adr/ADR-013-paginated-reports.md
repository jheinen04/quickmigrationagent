ADR-013: Pixel-Perfect Paginated Reports StrategyStatusProposedDate2026-09-08Decision MakersEnterprise ArchitectBI Platform LeadBusiness Reporting LeadUX/Design LeadContext and Problem StatementAmazon Quick Sight Pixel Perfect Reports enable organizations to create, schedule, and share highly formatted multipage PDF reports and CSV data exports directly from the Quick Sight web interface. This capability unifies historically separate systems for interactive dashboards and formatted reporting into a single platform. Report creators can connect to the full range of supported data sources and specify exact page dimensions, layout, and positioning of images, charts, and tables with pixel-level precision. Each report can generate a PDF of up to 1,000 pages.Pixel-perfect reports are designed to be printed or distributed — content is formatted to fit exact paper sizes and displays all data in tables and pivot tables even when spanning multiple pages. This is fundamentally different from interactive dashboards, which are designed for on-screen exploration.
