ADR-007: Region Selection and Multi-Account StrategyStatusProposedDate2026-09-08Decision MakersEnterprise ArchitectCloud Platform LeadCompliance OfficerNetwork ArchitectContext and Problem StatementAmazon Quick Sight is a regional service — the account is registered to a home region, SPICE capacity is allocated per region, and identity management is tied to a specific identity region. Organizations with global teams, data sovereignty requirements, or disaster recovery needs must decide on their region strategy and whether to use single or multiple AWS accounts for different environments. This decision has cascading effects on data residency compliance, latency for end users, SPICE capacity allocation, and cross-region data access patterns.Decision DriversData residency and sovereignty requirements (GDPR, HIPAA, local regulations)Geographic distribution of end users and acceptable latencySPICE capacity allocation strategy (capacity is per-region)Cross-region data access requirementsMulti-account strategy for environment isolation (Dev/Test/Prod)Identity region constraints (identity management is tied to a specific region)Service availability in target regionsConsidered Options7a.
